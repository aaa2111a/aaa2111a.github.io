About

Anti Bot 2000 is an off-chain whitelist builder. It curates your community by on-chain behavior — filtering fresh, empty and farmed wallets — instead of a raffle or a CAPTCHA.

For creators
It's invite-only

Request a creator code on Twitter. A code is single-use — it unlocks one action: open a room, or scan a whitelist.

Open a room

Set the wallet requirements and a timer, then share your link. People join live with their wallet — pass the bar and they're added. When it closes or fills, export the qualified wallets straight to an OpenSea allowlist.

Scan a whitelist

Already have a list? Upload a CSV of wallets and get back only the ones that clear your requirements — the clean list, ready to download. A display-only showcase card also publishes on the home page with your collection and the number of wallets you scanned.

For collectors
Joining a room

Open a room link, paste your wallet address, and it's scanned on-chain, live. Clear the requirements and you're on the list. No sign-up, no wallet connection — you paste an address, nothing is signed.

What the scan looks at

Creators pick any mix of these — real wallets clear them, throwaway wallets don't:

  • Average hold time (anti-flipper)
  • Wallet age
  • Minimum mints
  • Minimum transactions
  • Distinct collections
  • Active across months
  • Diamond hands (% still held)
  • Must hold a collection
  • Paid mints only
  • Ignore fresh activity
FAQ
Is this a CAPTCHA?

No. It doesn't prove there's a human — it reads a wallet's on-chain history to filter fresh, empty and farmed wallets. A determined person with a real, aged, funded wallet can still pass; the point is to raise the cost far above a throwaway.

How do I get a creator code?

Request one on Twitter — @unbearableknife. Codes are invite-only and single-use: one code opens one room or runs one scan.

What's the difference between a room and a CSV scan?

A room is a live link people join with their own wallet, in real time. A scan takes a list you already have (a CSV) and hands back only the wallets that pass — no link, no waiting for people to join.

What CSV format should I use?

Any plain .csv or .txt file of Ethereum addresses (0x…, 40 hex characters). One per line, or separated by commas, semicolons or spaces — all work, and you can mix them. Headers and extra columns are ignored; only the wallet addresses are read, and duplicates are removed automatically. Up to 5,000 wallets, file up to 10 MB. ENS names aren't supported — use the 0x address.

Can bots fake the requirements?

Some are cheap to fake — a bot can self-deploy throwaway contracts and mint to itself. Others can't: "active across months" needs real time, "paid mints only" needs real ETH to have left the wallet. Stack a few and faking gets expensive fast. It's a strong filter, not a guarantee.

Is my wallet data stored?

The scan reads only public on-chain data. A room's whitelist is deleted 5 days after the room finishes — creators download it in time; the room itself stays as a record. Wallet lists are never public.

What's a showcase?

When a creator scans a CSV, a display-only card publishes on the home page — the collection name, banner, and the number of wallets scanned. It's not joinable; it's just a public record of the scan.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Creating is gated by invite (a code), not by payment. Joining a room is free too.
